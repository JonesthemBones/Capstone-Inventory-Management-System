Inventory Guide

To track, update, and manage the stock items.


Overview

  • This module lists all store items with their details including stock levels, unit prices, and status.

Search & Filter

  • Use Search, Category, and Stock Status for common lookups. Choose "More Filters" when you need to filter by unit, availability, quantity, or selling price. The number beside the button shows how many additional filters are active.
  • Use Previous and Next below the product list to move between pages, or change how many items appear on each page.

Actions

  • The action buttons allow you to view product history, edit details, or delete items.

Add or Update Items

  • To add a new product, click "Add Item," choose its category, and complete the form. When editing a product, you can also move it to a different category. To take items out of inventory, use "Remove Stock" and enter the quantity and reason.

Status Indicators

  • Colored badges indicate stock status: Green for In Stock, Yellow for Low Stock, and Red for Out of Stock.

Product Images

  • Click on any product thumbnail to view it in full size. Click the placeholder to upload a product image for easy identification.

⚠️ Always record why stock was changed, and review every quantity update for accuracy. ⚠️
