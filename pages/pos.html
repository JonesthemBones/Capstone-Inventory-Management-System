Sales Checkout Guide

Use this page to sell products and give the customer a receipt.

How do I make a sale?

  • Search for a product, add it to the cart, check the quantity, then choose Checkout.

How do I find an item?

  • Search by product name or code, or use the Category filter to browse a product group.

What if an item cannot be added?

  • Check its available stock. Out-of-stock products cannot be added to the cart.

How do I correct a completed sale?

  • Open today's transactions, view the sale, and use Void Transaction with a clear reason.
