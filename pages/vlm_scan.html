Receipt Scanner Guide

            

                
Turn a supplier receipt into inventory items without typing every line manually.

                
How do I scan a receipt?
  • Take or upload a clear photo, choose Scan Receipt, and wait for the items to appear.

                
What should I review?
  • Check every product name, quantity, cost, category, and selling price before approval.

                
What if the scan is incorrect?
  • Edit incorrect values or remove unwanted items. A clearer, well-lit photo can improve the result.
